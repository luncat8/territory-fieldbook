Model reference

Field guide

Supply & storage
Each source produces units per simulated second while its cell has space. Amber shows actual stock on a log scale so small shipments stay visible.
Demand & intake
Consumers raise a demand peak that diffuses into neighboring cells. Stock climbs the field using a filtered direction. A consumer uses at most × local demand per second. New arrivals enter a quarter-second intake buffer; small deliveries drain gradually rather than vanishing on contact. Excess stays upstream.
Catchment feedback
Off by default, this mode lets a consumer read the stock weighted around it within four cells, so coal queued just outside its cell still cools demand. Demand falls as 1 / (1 + 3 × stock) there, on top of the intake-queue response. It answers the slow-signal question without replacing the queue: admission and service stay exactly as they are.
Pressure without consumers
Resource pressure lowers demand where stock accumulates. On a zero-demand plateau it spreads stock toward emptier neighbors instead. Pause, choose Fill 0, clear consumers, then resume. Compare pressure on and off without a leftover demand gradient.
Reading the experiment
The opening view is a 12-second reference run. Either reset starts at time zero, keeping your pressure and playback settings. Rates are per simulated second. Arrows show actual stock transfers; purple contours join equal demand. Select a cell to pin its readings.
