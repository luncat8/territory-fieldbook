Field guide

Worker wallets & fresh pools
Commuting and depleted workers carry conserved gold fields alongside their cell counts. Firms size an on-site fresh-worker pool to real shift occupancy ( u/s base mine rate), emitting wage demand inversely with pool occupancy down to zero on overflow. The wage is paid into the wallet when a worker enters the pool, so later overflow cannot cut it; an empty purse leaves back-pay owed.
Towns, wait pools & living tax
Towns do not route workers; tired workers climb a home field that trades rest quality against living tax, weighted by their own wallet (poor workers favour cheap, rich ones comfort). A town only rests workers for whom it is the local best home and raises living tax when its wait pool crowds. Resting into fresh labor requires paying the town's gold fee; workers standing still away from towns rest slowly for free (the shelter). Land sets the rest pace: meadow rests best, desert and the wasteland around mines and factories worst. A tired worker standing still beyond every town's reach slowly decides to settle; where enough decided workers gather, on the best land away from facilities and other towns, a settlement is founded — a young town that fells nearby forest for its first build, then grows with bought comfort; it keeps a lower rest speed, tax, bids and housing while under construction. Starter towns bid g/u for goods and use goods/s. Each town draws one of three wealth strategies (Cheap / Med / Rich) that decide how it taxes and grows: Cheap fills vacant slots by dropping tax and grows when the wait pool queues; Med is balanced; Rich holds a few free slots, raises tax when crowded and cuts size if it cannot meet needs.
Land, sawmills, farms & waste
Ground decides how easily a town builds and how many it can house: shores are best, then forest–meadow glades, meadow, hills, forest, swamp edges and swamp. Water blocks every flow. Sawmills hire workers to fell surrounding forest patches into priced wood, while farms clear nearby moist meadow or shore plots and drop seasonal food harvests that walk up gold-backed food bids. Towns also forage wild food and wood with idle residents when commercial stock runs short. Resident workers move on their own, split when fed with spare housing and savings, and starve when unfed. Consumed stock and farm fertilizer leave CO2 and litter that heal slowly.
Factories & sell floors
Factories never pause conversion of owned stock. Input book cost carries into finished goods and sets a minimum sell price so stocked product is never sold at a loss; buy prices adapt to output margin and profit.
Gold & payments
Gold is conserved across municipal vaults, resident purses, mobile worker wallets and seller payment streams. Gold mines start at g/s and expand while local wages stay profitable.
Price decay
Diffused bids, wages and town appeal fade with distance to emulate transport and migration cost. Switch it off and signals reach the whole map at full strength.
Map edges
Wrap · torus joins the left and right rim and the top and bottom rim, so goods, workers and prices cross the seams and no corner is special. Bounded keeps hard edges: corner cells miss neighbours, so remnant price peaks linger in the corners and pull stock there.
