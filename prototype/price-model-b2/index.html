Field guide

Worker wallets & fresh pools
Commuting and depleted workers carry conserved gold fields alongside their cell counts. Firms size an on-site fresh-worker pool to real shift occupancy ( u/s base mine rate), emitting wage demand inversely with pool occupancy down to zero on overflow. The wage is paid into the wallet when a worker enters the pool, so later overflow cannot cut it; an empty purse leaves back-pay owed.
Towns, wait pools & living tax
Towns do not route workers; tired workers climb a home field that trades rest quality against living tax, weighted by their own wallet (poor workers favour cheap, rich ones comfort). A town only rests workers for whom it is the local best home and raises living tax when its wait pool crowds. Resting into fresh labor requires paying the town's gold fee; workers standing still away from towns rest slowly for free (the shelter). Where enough of them gather beyond any town's reach a settlement is founded — a young town that fells nearby forest for its first build, then grows with bought comfort; it keeps a lower rest speed, tax, bids and housing while under construction. Starter towns bid g/u for goods and use goods/s. Each town draws one of three wealth strategies (Cheap / Med / Rich) that decide how it taxes and grows: Cheap fills vacant slots by dropping tax and grows when the wait pool queues; Med is balanced; Rich holds a few free slots, raises tax when crowded and cuts size if it cannot meet needs.
Factories & sell floors
Factories never pause conversion of owned stock. Input book cost carries into finished goods and sets a minimum sell price so stocked product is never sold at a loss; buy prices adapt to output margin and profit.
Gold & payments
Gold is conserved across municipal vaults, resident purses, mobile worker wallets and seller payment streams. Gold mines start at g/s and expand while local wages stay profitable.
Price decay
Diffused bids, wages and town appeal fade with distance to emulate transport and migration cost. Switch it off and signals reach the whole map at full strength.
