Field guide

Goods particles & Transport Company
Producers batch output ( u/s base mine rate) into SoA particles once uphill bids clear cost basis. Moving particles accrue terrain-effort transport cost and warm in colour. On buyer admission, min(buyerPay, transportCost) enters the map-wide Transport Company vault (dripped back to active towns) and the remainder pays the owner purse.
Worker particles, rooms & wealth classes
Every person is a persistent worker particle with a wallet, stamina and food bag. Firms prepay shift wages at pool entry; tired workers steer by a wallet-weighted pareto blend of rest value and quality, paying check-in tax in town rooms ( g/u starter goods bid, u/s town need) across Cheap, Med and Rich strategies.
Self-foraging & Monastery gardens & worker survival
Towns never spawn or despawn workers. A worker can split every 45 seconds when most needs are met, it is well rested, has a nearly full food bag and at least 16 gold; wallet and food are shared. Only 120 seconds without food causes despawning. When a worker's food bag runs low and he cannot buy rations in town, he enters dedicated Forage mode (ignoring job, home and founding tasks), climbs a coarse half-resolution food gradient updated every 4 ticks toward water shorelines, forests and Monastery gardens, gathers free food (0 gold) and returns to work. Monastery gardens farm surrounding plots on seasonal cycles to stock free charity rations.
Food farms
High food bids and free fresh workers found farms where forest or wood can build them. Paid crews clear random cells completely into farmland (one worker per cell), then harvest every seven seconds. Humidity sets yield at harvest; small waste goes to one random cell outside the farm, even on neighboring farmland. Town bazaars buy this food and sell it to workers; goods provide comfort, not food.
Gold & founding
Gold is conserved across purses, worker wallets and the Transport Company vault ( g/s base gold mine). Still tired workers beyond town reach found young towns on shoreline/meadow land; still fresh workers found new firms — neither mints gold.
