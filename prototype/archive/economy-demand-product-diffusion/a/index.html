Model notes

Mines and settlements placed with the map tools are added to the same fields as a generated layout; Erase removes the nearest one. Stock feedback measures the whole catchment, so coal queued just outside a settlement still lowers its demand.

Demand diffuses with reflecting edges and slowly fades. Settlements consume at a rate proportional to demand at their center, capped by available coal in their catchment. Coal outside the catchment must travel there first. Stock feedback reduces the settlement signal, while fixed demand leaves it unchanged.

Uphill transfers conserve coal, but the grid discretization spreads it as it travels. Color saturates at 10 coal per cell; use the peak and hover readouts for larger values. The balance error checks produced minus consumed against stored coal. Simulation speed may fall behind on slow devices rather than catch up after a stalled frame.
