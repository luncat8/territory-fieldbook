    Particle transfer reference

    Field guide

    Owned goods particles & cost basis
    Mines ( u/s base) and factories accumulate output in cell spawn buffers and launch a particle only when an uphill price gradient clears selfPrice (exact input + wage book cost plus margin). In transit, haulage cost accrues as 1 / conductance over meadow, forest and desert, raising each particle's unit price color from green-gold toward violet.
    Arrival split & Transport Company
    Buyers admit arriving particles up to remaining buffer room. From the buyer's payment, accrued haulage settles to the map-wide Transport Company vault (which gently pays carrier dividends back to active town households) and the remainder credits directly to the particle's producing owner, realizing exact profit amount · (buyerPrice − item.price). Gold mines start at g/s.
    Worker particles & pools
    Fresh workers leave town docks as walkers climbing the wage bid and join a firm's pool on arrival, where the entry wage is paid into their wallet. Worked shifts turn tired: tired walkers carry their wallets up the home appeal (broke ones chase cheap rest, funded ones rest quality) and join a town's wait pool unless a neighbor offers better. Walkers queued at a full pool cool its wage and raise its crowd tax; still walkers settle where they stand, rest slowly and may found a town.
    Underwater consignments, towns & land
    If local price drops mid-flight, shipments re-steer toward hungrier buyers; at a local price peak below delivered cost, non-perishables wait in consignment while perishables spot-sell before spoiling. Starter towns bid g/u for goods and use goods/s, taxing worker rest by wealth class while land rest and town claims govern new settlement founding.
    Conservation ledgers

    Coal 0 mined − 0 burned − 0 spoiled = 0 total (0 in flight)

    Goods 0 made − 0 eaten − 0 spoiled = 0 total (0 in flight)

    Labor 0 supplied − 0 worked = 0 total (0 walking)

    Depleted 0 tired − 0 settled = 0 total (0 walking)

    Gold 0 seeded + 0 mined = 0 total (0 haul vault · 0 worker wallets)
