Model reference

Field guide

Mines & stock
Each mine produces units of coal per simulated second while the coal price at its cell beats the gate. Amber shows actual stock on a log scale so small shipments stay visible.
Towns & bids
A town bids for goods at up to 4 $/u, cooled by the goods already on hand. The bid diffuses across the map and weakens with distance, so distant stock is worth less. A town eats at most units per second; new arrivals enter a quarter-second intake buffer.
Factories & derived bids
A factory burns coal into goods. Its coal bid is derived from the goods price at its own cell: 60% of the output price, cooled by coal and goods already on hand. Dear goods nearby pull coal in; cheap goods starve the plant.
Price decay
Diffused price fades as it travels, which stands in for transport cost. Switch it off and bids reach the whole map at full strength. A mine beyond the faded price idles instead of producing into a void.
Reading the experiment
The opening view is an 8-second reference run. Reset starts at time zero. Arrows show actual stock transfers; purple contours join equal price. Select a cell to pin its readings.
